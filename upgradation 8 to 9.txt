																======================

																Create the mqm group (on UNIX/Linux).

																Disk space check
																=================

																Verify enough free space in /var/mqm (Linux/UNIX) or C:\Program Files\IBM\MQ (Windows).

																TCP and IP
																IP (Internet Protocol):

																Think of it as the address system of the internet.

																It defines how data packets are addressed and routed from one computer to another.

																Example: Your home address tells the post office where to deliver a letter; IP addresses tell the network where to deliver data.

																TCP (Transmission Control Protocol):

																Works on top of IP.

																Ensures that data is delivered reliably and in the correct order.

																It breaks large messages into smaller packets, sends them, and reassembles them at the destination.

																If packets are lost, TCP requests them again.
